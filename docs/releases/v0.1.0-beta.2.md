Second test build. Starting it is now just a double-click, plus a big batch of new features. Thanks for all the feedback on beta.1!

## Install / update

1. Download **RSOverlay-v0.1.0-beta.2-windows-x64.zip** below and unzip it. Updating? Just replace your old `rsoverlay.exe`. Your settings and layouts are kept.
2. **Double-click `rsoverlay.exe`.** With SteamVR and your headset available it starts in VR; otherwise it opens the desktop settings window. No more `--vr`, and no more console window.
3. SteamVR keeps starting RSOverlay by itself (toggle in SteamVR › Settings › Startup / Shutdown).

If you have **Settings › Updates › Include test builds** on, RSOverlay tells you when the next test build is out.

## New

**Easier to start and to report problems**
- Starts in VR on double-click; no console window
- Settings › Updates › **Report a problem** and **Open logs** (please attach `rsoverlay.log` to bug reports)

**Working with windows**
- Desktop page › **Big screen**: your main monitor as one large curved screen in front of you
- Desktop page › **All monitors** (2+ monitors): every monitor on an arc around you, left to right like your desk
- **Size** menu on window toolbars: resize the real window from VR (smaller window = bigger text)
- Sideways scrolling with the stick
- **Touch input for windows** (Settings › Pointer): the trigger acts like a finger, for touch-friendly apps
- **Mouse follows the laser** can be turned off (the cursor then only moves when you click or drag)

**Wrist**
- **Focus** button: notifications wait in history, and every window except the one you're working in dims
- **Background image**: drop any PNG / JPG into the backgrounds folder (Settings › Wrist toolbar › Open folder)
- Clock **12 h / 24 h** and date format

**Comfort and controls**
- **Vibration** strength and **Grab smoothing** (Settings › Pointer)
- **Gamepad shortcuts** for seated / sim play: Back+LB dashboard, Back+RB recenter, Back+Y keyboard (Settings › Gamepad, off by default)
- VR keyboard: tap the **language pill** to switch between your Windows keyboard layouts
- **Unwatched panels** refresh rate (Settings › VR panels): lower saves GPU, higher keeps a video smooth out of the corner of your eye

**Notifications and batteries**
- **Mute** an app from the notification history (its messages still land in history, just silently)
- Hide battery devices you don't care about (Battery card › Devices)

## Please test

- Double-click start: does it come up in VR every time? Without a headset, do you get the settings window?
- Big screen / All monitors: placement, size, readability
- Focus mode: does the right window stay bright?
- Touch input on an app you use with touch
- Gamepad shortcuts if you play seated with a controller

## Experimental

- **Block game input while pointing** (Settings › Pointer, off by default): the game shouldn't get your trigger / button presses while the laser is on a panel. SteamVR may show its own pointer while it's active. Please tell us how it behaves.

## Known issues

- Everything above is new; most of it hasn't had much headset time yet.
- Most of the interface is still English, even with another language selected.
